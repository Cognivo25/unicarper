## The Aggregator Advantage
One plan. One coordination layer. One accountable team.

### The Problem
Projects slow down when designers, vendors, contractors, and site teams work separately. Scope changes, rework, and delays become expensive.

### The Unicarper Role
Unicarper coordinates design, procurement, vendors, site crews, and project managers under one delivery plan.

### The Outcome
Owners get clearer scope, better coordination, controlled execution, and smoother handover — without chasing multiple teams.
Across interiors, outdoor environments, pools, spas, landscapes, water features, security infrastructure, and PMC-led projects.

## Our Verticals
Six streams—interior, outdoor, pools and spas, landscape water, security, and PMC—under one programme with clear scope and coordinated delivery.

### Interior Design-Build
Interior concepts, material direction, fit-outs, and execution support for residential and commercial spaces.
Coordinated from design intent through site delivery.

### Outdoor Environments
Landscaping, hardscape, and outdoor living tied to the building envelope, drainage, and material sequencing.
Levels, procurement, and installation dates aligned to the live site.

### Pools, Spas & Jacuzzis
Fiberglass pools, spa shells, jacuzzis, and outdoor wellness installations.
Product selection, site planning, and installation coordination.

### Landscape & Water Features
Water walls, cascades, fountains, and engineered water bodies scoped with landscape and architecture.
Hydraulics, circulation, and finishes handled as one package.

### Security Infrastructure & Surveillance
CCTV, access paths, cable routes, and rack-room layouts coordinated with civil and MEP work.
Residential towers, commercial blocks, and large-format campuses.

### PMC Services
Schedules, vendor cadence, site reporting, and change control under a visible programme.
Budget and timeline stay in view through execution.
